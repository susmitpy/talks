# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Technical conference audiences—primarily software engineers, architects, and cloud practitioners—who need to understand a production workflow pattern quickly in a projected, time-boxed talk.

## Product Purpose

Maintain a collection of clear, memorable Slidev presentations that teach one technical story at a time, support live delivery, and leave audiences with practical guidance they can apply after the session.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, pragmatic, and technically credible. Talks should feel lively and approachable without sacrificing precision.

## Anti-references

Dense documentation pasted onto slides, generic corporate templates, low-contrast decoration, unstyled diagrams, gratuitous gradients, and visual effects that compete with the speaker.

## Design Principles

- Lead with a strong narrative: problem, complication, solution, and practical limits.
- Keep one idea per slide and reveal complexity progressively.
- Use visuals to clarify the technical mechanism, not merely to decorate it.
- Make every key claim legible from the back of a conference room.
- Balance enthusiasm with honest tradeoffs and implementation caveats.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use high-contrast text, readable type sizes, redundant cues beyond color, restrained motion with reduced-motion support, and concise language suitable for a mixed-experience audience.
